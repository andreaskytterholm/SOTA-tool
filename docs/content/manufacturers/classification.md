# Classification of medical devices

Medical devices are classified based on their intended use, complexity,
and potential risk to patients and users. This classification is crucial
because it determines the regulatory requirements a device must meet
before it can be marketed and used in healthcare settings.

Classification helps manufacturers understand the regulatory pathway
early in the development process.
